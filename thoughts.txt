DLPNO-CCSD(T):
Now I am starting the DLPNO-CCSD(T), Domain-based Local Pair Natural Orbital Coupled Cluster Singles and Doubles (Pertubrative Triples). Basically this will give me a more accurate electronic energies which I can use to calculate activation energy (Ea) = E_TS - (E_reactants) or reaction energy (Erxn) = E_products - E_reactants at the electronic energy level. 

I will start with optimized atoms from the IRC calculations to calculate DLPNO as it is usually done in different manuscripts because it saved computational cost and we can use higher level of theory for energy calculations. Basis set i am choosing is "cc-pVTZ" because it is middle of the pack and gives accurate calculations without being computationally expensive. 

I also have to choose PNO which is the approximation of the general CCSD(T). I am choosing "TightPNO" which is the more accurate and computationally costly. Keeping it high because I want to see accurate energies for my MESS reaction rate calculations.
